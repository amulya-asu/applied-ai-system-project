- `Dance on Beat` versus `Silent Getaway`: The EDM profile produced high-energy songs like `Pixel Rush`, while the ambient profile shifted toward calmer songs like `Spacewalk Thoughts` and `Blue Window`. This makes sense because the biggest difference between these profiles is the target energy level and overall mood.

- `Dance on Beat` versus `Midnight Steel`: Both profiles preferred high-energy songs, so their outputs overlapped in intensity, but the top result changed from EDM to metal. This makes sense because both users want strong energy, but their preferred genres and moods are different.

- `Silent Getaway` versus `Midnight Steel`: These profiles produced the biggest contrast. `Silent Getaway` favored low-energy, chill songs, while `Midnight Steel` favored loud, intense tracks like `Iron Skyline` and `Storm Runner`. This makes sense because the profiles are testing opposite ends of the energy and mood range.
